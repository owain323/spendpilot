What the ledger counts

ALERTS

Something looks wrong. Spend rose without value behind it — shown to you, never acted on by itself.

ACTIONS

Did something. Proposed with proof, approved by your session, executed — each step gets its own entry.

HELD

Deliberately quiet. Low confidence or already surfaced — held back or not repeated, with the reason logged.

REFUSED

Said no. A mandate was forged, expired, replayed, or drifted past its cap — refused and recorded.
